How Task Buddy uses AI

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Task Buddy's "buddy" uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. This page explains, in plain language, what is sent to Anthropic, when, what happens to it, and what control you have. It is part of our Privacy Policy.

When anything is sent

Only when you use an AI feature, for example turning a sentence, voice note or photo into tasks, breaking a task into steps, asking for a daily plan or review, chatting with the buddy, or receiving an AI check-in you turned on. Using Task Buddy as a normal to-do list sends nothing to Anthropic.

What is sent

Kind What is sent What is not sent
Text What you typed into the AI feature, plus the tasks needed for the request (titles, notes, steps, due dates, priorities of the tasks involved) Your email address or account details
Voice The text transcript of what you said The audio recording. Speech is turned into text by your phone (on the device where available, otherwise by Apple's or Google's speech service)
Images The photo you chose for that request Any other photo in your library

How it gets there

The app never talks to Anthropic directly. Requests go to Task Buddy's own server, which checks that you are signed in, applies fair-use limits, sends the request to Anthropic's API and checks the answer before returning it. Our server does not store the content of your requests; it keeps only a usage record (time, feature, size and cost).

Retention and training

  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms for its API, Anthropic may not use content we send it to train its models.
  • Anthropic's published policy for API customers (checked 28 October 2026) is to delete inputs and outputs automatically within 30 days. Content flagged by its automated safety systems as violating its Usage Policy may be kept for up to 2 years, and data may be kept longer where the law requires.
  • Sources: Anthropic commercial terms, Anthropic commercial data retention.

Your control

  • AI never changes your tasks by itself: you see each suggestion and confirm it first.
  • You can use Task Buddy without any AI feature.
  • Avoid putting sensitive information (for example health, financial or ID details) into AI features if you do not want it sent to Anthropic.
  • To delete your account and the data we hold about you, see Deleting your account.

Limits of AI

AI suggestions can be wrong. Check dates, times and steps before relying on them. Task Buddy is a planning aid, not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.
