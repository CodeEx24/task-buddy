4. AI features and what is sent to the AI provider

Task Buddy's AI features are provided by Anthropic (the company that makes Claude). The app never contacts Anthropic directly: every AI request goes from the app to our own server first, which checks that you are signed in, applies usage limits, and then forwards the request. Anthropic's API key is kept on our server and is never in the app.

What is sent, and when

  • Text: the sentence you type or paste into an AI feature, and when you ask for a plan, a breakdown, a review or a check-in, the tasks needed for that request (for example titles, notes, steps, due dates and priorities of the tasks involved).
  • Voice: only the text transcript of what you said. The audio is not sent to us or to Anthropic.
  • Images: only the photo you chose for that request.
  • Nothing is sent to Anthropic when you use Task Buddy without an AI feature. Your email address is not sent to Anthropic.

How Anthropic handles it

Anthropic processes this content as our service provider under its commercial terms for its API. Under those terms Anthropic may not use it to train its models. Anthropic's published policy for API customers (checked 28 October 2026) is that inputs and outputs are automatically deleted from its systems within 30 days, with exceptions: content its automated safety systems flag as violating its Usage Policy may be kept for up to 2 years, and data may be kept longer where the law requires. See Anthropic's commercial terms and Anthropic's commercial data retention policy.

What we keep

Our server does not store the text or photos you send to an AI feature. It keeps a usage record (time, feature, size and cost, not the content) to apply limits. AI suggestions are shown to you first and are only saved to your tasks if you confirm them; saved suggestions are stored with your tasks on your device.

AI output can be wrong. Please check suggested dates, times and steps before relying on them. More detail is in the AI disclosure.

6. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that run parts of Task Buddy for us:

  • Supabase: sign-in, account database, our server functions.
  • Anthropic: AI features (section 4).
  • Sentry: crash reporting.
  • PostHog: optional analytics, only if you opt in.
  • Expo: app updates, and push notification delivery once check-ins launch.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution, speech recognition when it is not done on the device, and push notification delivery.

Task Buddy is free and has no payments today. If we add a paid plan, payments will be handled by the App Store or Google Play, with subscription status managed by RevenueCat, and we will update this policy before that plan launches. We would never see your card details.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect users or the service, or as part of a transfer of the app to a new operator, who would have to honour this policy.

7. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in the providers' data processing terms.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account (email, account ID): until you delete your account. Deletion from backups may take up to 30 more days.
  • Tasks on your device: until you uninstall the app. A task you delete is hidden at once and marked as deleted in the app's database on your device (this is what makes undo work); it never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall.
  • AI content: not stored on our server. Anthropic's retention is described in section 4.
  • AI usage records: up to 13 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Server request logs: our server writes a technical log line for each AI request (time, feature, result, model, duration and size; never the content, your email or your account ID). These operational logs are deleted automatically at the end of Supabase's log retention period for our plan.
  • Crash reports: according to Sentry's retention for our plan, at most 90 days.
  • Optional analytics: up to 13 months after collection.

To make a request, contact us at the address in section 1. We reply within 30 days and may need to confirm that you own the account.

Deleting your account

An in-app option to delete your account is coming in a future update. Until then, email us from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your account and the data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. Tasks stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the app.
